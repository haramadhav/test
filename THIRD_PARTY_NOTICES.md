## FreeSurfer reference geometry

The brain graphic uses the FreeSurfer fsaverage5 pial template, as distributed
with Nilearn. The reference geometry was modified by rescaling and reorienting it
for this rendering.

Sources:
- https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/tree/main/nilearn/datasets/data/fsaverage5
- https://nilearn.github.io/stable/modules/generated/nilearn.datasets.load_fsaverage.html
- https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/fswiki/FreeSurferSoftwareLicense
